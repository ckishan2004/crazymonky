Trading platform

AlgoBridge

A self-hosted bridge between your charts and your broker. Signals arrive from TradingView, ChartInk or a spreadsheet; AlgoBridge checks them, sizes the order and sends it on. A stop engine sits on the server watching stop-loss, target and trailing levels, so your exits survive a dropped internet connection. There's a watchlist, chart trading, an orderbook, a positions view, a Telegram bot you can arm from your phone, and a visual builder for wiring up your own flow.

Pine Script strategy

Breakout Elite

Enters on an EMA50 cross, books part of the position at EMA20 and re-enters when the move continues — without ever letting the held quantity drift above the original size. Runs in intraday mode, which flattens at the square-off time and starts clean the next day, or positional mode, which keeps holding.

  • Pine Script v6
  • TradingView alerts
  • JSON payload
